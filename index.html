Our Story

Where beauty rituals meet modern care

Started in 2016 by a small team of beauticians trained in both classical Indian techniques and international styling, Aaina has grown into Bhubaneswar's trusted address for hair, skin, and bridal beauty.

We use dermat-approved products, sanitised tools, and take the time to actually listen — because every face, every skin type, and every occasion asks for something different.
